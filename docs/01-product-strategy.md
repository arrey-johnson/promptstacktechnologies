# 01 — Product Strategy

## Product statement

Build a sophisticated, credible, conversion-focused digital platform that positions Promptstack Technologies as a technology partner for African businesses, turns business problems into clear software, AI/automation, and digital-growth solutions, generates qualified commercial opportunities, and establishes Promptstack Academy as a practical technology education and talent-development platform.

## Primary success objective

Generate qualified opportunities for Promptstack Technologies.

The most important business conversion is not a page view or newsletter signup. It is a serious prospect who submits a project inquiry and enters the sales process.

## Secondary objective

Generate qualified Promptstack Academy applicants and enrolments.

## Supporting goals

- improve brand credibility;
- support referrals by giving prospects a professional place to evaluate Promptstack;
- make founder/social content convert into business interest;
- create useful sales collateral through case studies and Insights;
- build organic search visibility;
- support recruitment and partnerships;
- create a web architecture that can later connect to an LMS, client portal, or other products without putting those products in v1.

## Positioning

External working positioning:

> Promptstack Technologies helps businesses solve operational and growth problems through software, artificial intelligence, automation, and digital marketing.

Internal operating idea:

> We solve business problems with technology.

This means the site should not organize every message around technical capability. It should first establish the business problem and outcome.

## Strategic messaging hierarchy

1. Business outcome
2. Business problem
3. Promptstack solution area
4. Capability/service
5. Technical detail

Example:

Bad:
`React · Node.js · AI · SEO · Mobile apps`

Preferred:
`Your customer follow-up is getting lost → build a better customer-management process → software/automation solution → specific implementation capabilities`.

## Trust strategy

Because Promptstack is building its public market presence, trust must come from real evidence rather than manufactured authority.

Use:

- founder presence;
- real team;
- real project work;
- actual product screenshots;
- documented delivery process;
- case studies;
- useful expert content;
- Academy projects;
- real photography;
- transparent project methodology.

As they become available, add:

- client logos with permission;
- testimonials;
- verified quantitative results;
- partnerships;
- certifications.

Never fabricate social proof.

## Market stance

Promptstack is Cameroon-based and should be visually and commercially authentic to that environment, while remaining capable of serving wider African markets.

Avoid pretending the company already has a continent-wide footprint if that is not factually true.

## Commercial discipline

Only promote services that:

- solve clear customer problems;
- have identifiable buyers;
- can be scoped;
- can be priced profitably;
- Promptstack can deliver;
- can lead to repeat/recurring relationships;
- are relevant to the Cameroonian and wider African market.

The website should not become a catalog of everything a technology professional could theoretically do.
