# 05 — Page Requirements

## `/solutions`

Audience:
business visitor who knows they need improvement but is not sure which discipline.

Must communicate:
- the three solution areas;
- common problems each solves;
- how the areas may work together;
- links to Work and How We Work.

Primary CTA:
Start a Project.

## `/solutions/software`

Main question:
Can Promptstack build the system/platform my business needs?

Sections:
1. Hero/outcome
2. Problems custom software solves
3. Capabilities
4. Common use cases
5. Delivery approach
6. Relevant work
7. FAQ/objections
8. CTA

Capability examples:
- custom business software;
- management systems;
- web applications;
- websites/digital platforms;
- e-commerce;
- mobile applications;
- system integrations.

Do not lead with frameworks.

## `/solutions/ai-automation`

Main question:
What work can we automate and where can AI create practical value?

Sections:
1. Hero
2. Symptoms of automation opportunity
3. What Promptstack can automate
4. Practical AI use cases
5. Workflow/process examples
6. Delivery process
7. Relevant proof
8. FAQ
9. CTA

Avoid hype about replacing entire teams.

## `/solutions/digital-marketing`

Main question:
Can Promptstack turn digital activity into measurable acquisition/growth?

Sections:
1. Hero
2. Common growth problems
3. Strategy/capabilities
4. Lead-generation and conversion model
5. Reporting/measurement
6. Relevant work
7. Process
8. CTA

The differentiation should connect marketing to systems, data, automation, and measurable outcomes.

## `/work`

Purpose:
evidence.

Provide:
- featured case study;
- filter/category if enough real content exists;
- project cards;
- clear labeling of client work vs internal/demo/Academy work.

Do not add filters if there are too few items.

## `/work/[slug]`

Fields/sections:
1. Hero
2. Client/context
3. Problem
4. Why it mattered
5. Approach
6. Solution
7. Product/work gallery
8. Implementation
9. Outcome
10. Technology/services
11. Testimonial if real
12. Related work
13. CTA

Do not fabricate quantitative results.

## `/how-we-work`

Purpose:
risk reduction.

Sections:
- philosophy;
- Discover;
- Define;
- Design;
- Build;
- Launch;
- Improve;
- communication and project management;
- quality/testing;
- support;
- CTA.

The message: Promptstack does not prescribe technology before understanding the business.

## `/academy`

Purpose:
convert learner interest into program exploration/application.

Sections:
1. Learn · Build · Ship hero
2. What Promptstack Academy is
3. Learning model
4. Programs
5. What makes it practical
6. project/student proof when available
7. talent pipeline
8. learning environment
9. FAQ
10. Apply

## `/academy/programs`

Display real active programs only.

Each card:
- program name;
- short outcome;
- level;
- delivery mode;
- status/cohort if known;
- Explore CTA.

## `/academy/programs/[program]`

Sections:
1. Program promise
2. Who it is for
3. What learner will be able to do
4. Learning roadmap
5. Tools/technologies
6. Projects
7. How training works
8. instructor/support where approved
9. portfolio/professional outcomes
10. entry requirements
11. real fees/dates/delivery format
12. FAQ
13. Apply

Curriculum should communicate progression, not merely a technology list.

## `/academy/how-we-teach`

Explain:
- Learn · Build · Ship;
- practical projects;
- professional workflows;
- Agile/team practice where relevant;
- Git;
- documentation;
- testing;
- deployment;
- feedback;
- assessment.

## `/academy/apply`

Dedicated structured application.

Must not be a general contact form.

## `/insights`

Purpose:
authority + search + sales enablement.

Requirements:
- clear featured content;
- useful categories/tags;
- no empty category pages;
- search only when content volume justifies it later.

## `/insights/[slug]`

Requirements:
- readable long-form typography;
- author/date/category;
- proper heading structure;
- relevant Solution/Academy CTA;
- related content;
- SEO metadata;
- optimized social image.

## `/company/about`

Purpose:
explain why Promptstack exists and how the company is structured.

Sections:
- purpose;
- business positioning;
- divisions;
- operating philosophy;
- leadership/founder where approved;
- Academy relationship;
- market ambition;
- CTA.

Avoid long corporate autobiography.

## `/contact`

General inquiries:
- partnerships;
- administrative;
- suppliers;
- media;
- general messages.

Do not route project leads through this generic form when Start a Project exists.

## `/start-a-project`

Dedicated qualified business inquiry.

Core message:
Start with the business problem. Technical specification not required.

## Confirmation pages

Business:
- confirm receipt;
- explain next step;
- no false promise of a response deadline unless operations can meet it;
- optionally link to Work/Insights.

Academy:
- confirm application;
- explain review process;
- no false admission promise.
