# 04 — Homepage Specification and Approved Working Copy

## Homepage job

The homepage must:

- establish Promptstack as a serious technology company;
- help a business visitor recognize a real problem;
- explain the three commercial solution areas;
- communicate business outcomes;
- provide proof;
- reduce project risk through process;
- differentiate Promptstack;
- introduce Academy without diluting B2B focus;
- move qualified visitors to `Start a Project`.

## Section sequence

1. Header
2. Hero
3. Capability strip
4. Problem recognition
5. Solutions
6. Business outcomes
7. Selected work
8. How We Work
9. Why Promptstack
10. Academy
11. Insights
12. Final CTA
13. Footer

## Problem recognition

Working heading:

**Where is your business losing time, customers or visibility?**

Intro:

**Many businesses do not need more technology for the sake of technology. They need better ways to work, serve customers, make decisions and grow.**

Problem groups:

### Manual Operations
Important processes still depend on paper, spreadsheets, repeated data entry or constant manual coordination.

### Disconnected Information
Teams work from different sources of information, making it difficult to stay organized and make confident decisions.

### Lost Leads and Customers
Customer inquiries, sales opportunities and follow-ups are not consistently tracked.

### Repetitive Work
Employees spend valuable time doing work that software or automation could handle.

### Poor Business Visibility
Decision-makers struggle to get a clear picture of what is happening across the business.

### Weak Digital Growth
Marketing activity exists, but results are inconsistent, difficult to measure or disconnected from actual business growth.

## Selected Work

Heading:

**Selected Work**

Supporting direction:

**Problems become more convincing when you can show what you built to solve them.**

Use 2–4 real selected projects from CMS.

Recommended layout:
- one dominant project;
- up to two smaller supporting projects.

Each preview should show:
- project/client name;
- category;
- problem;
- solution;
- outcome where verified;
- real image/screenshot;
- View Project CTA.

Never invent measurable results.

## How We Work

Heading:

**From business problem to working solution.**

### 01 — Discover
We understand your business, workflow, users, goals and the problem you are trying to solve.

### 02 — Define
We turn the problem into clear requirements, priorities, scope and measurable outcomes.

### 03 — Design
We design the system, experience, workflow or campaign before committing to implementation.

### 04 — Build
We develop, integrate, test and improve the solution through structured iterations.

### 05 — Launch
We deploy the solution, verify that it works properly and support adoption.

### 06 — Improve
Where appropriate, we measure performance and continue improving the solution as the business evolves.

CTA:

**See How We Work**
