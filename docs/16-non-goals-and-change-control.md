# 16 — Non-Goals and Change Control

## Explicit v1 non-goals

Do not build:
- student LMS;
- client portal;
- public account system;
- talent marketplace;
- public student profiles;
- AI website chatbot;
- complex site search;
- advanced personalization;
- full bilingual/multilingual architecture unless separately approved;
- complex booking system unless sales process requires it;
- community forum;
- real-time chat;
- WebGL/3D experiences;
- excessive motion system;
- large service directory.

## Why

The initial website should remain focused on:
- credibility;
- sales;
- Academy acquisition;
- proof;
- authority;
- operational reliability.

Each non-goal can become a separate future product decision with its own business case.

## Change request test

A new idea must specify:
1. user;
2. problem;
3. expected outcome;
4. metric;
5. implementation cost;
6. ongoing maintenance;
7. launch impact;
8. whether it creates a new privacy/security burden.

Reject vague additions such as:
- “competitors have it”;
- “it looks cool”;
- “AI websites should have it”.

## Benchmark rule

Benchmarks inspire quality, not scope.

Ainet:
use as a reference for:
- clean light presentation;
- whitespace;
- strong typography;
- modern technology composition;
- restrained animation.

Do not copy:
- its desktop navigation;
- its identity;
- its page structure;
- its copy;
- its code;
- its generic AI look.

Promptstack must remain its own brand.
