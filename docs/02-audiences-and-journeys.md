# 02 — Audiences and User Journeys

## Business audience

The most important visitor is a decision-maker responsible for improving a business.

Typical roles:
- founder;
- owner;
- managing director;
- operations manager;
- administrator;
- marketing manager;
- department head;
- executive.

Typical thoughts:
- "We are losing track of customers."
- "Everything is manual."
- "Our staff waste time."
- "We need a proper system."
- "Our website is not producing business."
- "We cannot tell what marketing is working."
- "Customers keep asking the same questions."
- "Our information is scattered."

The user should not need to know the words `workflow automation`, `CRM`, or `custom platform` before Promptstack can help.

## Business persuasion journey

### Stage 1 — Arrival
Possible sources:
- Google;
- founder/social content;
- referral;
- WhatsApp;
- LinkedIn;
- paid advertising;
- event;
- direct search.

### Stage 2 — Recognition
Answer:
- who Promptstack is;
- what it helps with;
- why that matters to a business.

### Stage 3 — Problem recognition
Help the user see their own operational/growth issue.

### Stage 4 — Solution discovery
Introduce:
- Software Solutions;
- AI & Automation;
- Digital Marketing.

### Stage 5 — Evaluation/trust
Show:
- work;
- process;
- people;
- useful thinking;
- real outcomes.

### Stage 6 — Risk reduction
Answer:
- Will they understand my business?
- What happens after I contact them?
- Will I understand the project?
- What happens after launch?
- What if I do not know the technical solution?

Core reassurance:

> You do not need to arrive with a technical specification. Start with the business problem.

### Stage 7 — Conversion
Primary action: Start a Project.

## Academy audience

### Beginner
Needs confidence, structure, and clear prerequisites.

### University student
Needs practical competence, projects, collaboration, and professional workflows beyond theory.

### Career changer/professional
Needs practical relevance and a credible path to useful skills.

### Ambitious emerging technologist
Wants serious capability, not merely a certificate.

## Academy journey

1. Discover Promptstack Academy
2. Understand `Learn · Build · Ship`
3. Find a relevant program
4. Understand what they will be able to do
5. See how training works
6. See project/student proof
7. Understand expectations and opportunity pathways
8. Apply

## Academy promise discipline

Do not promise employment.

Acceptable positioning:

> Strong learners may be considered for internships, apprenticeships, freelance project teams, or junior opportunities within the Promptstack ecosystem when opportunities are available.

Do not imply that completing a course guarantees a job.

## Learn · Build · Ship methodology

### Learn
Concepts, demonstrations, instructor guidance, exercises, fundamentals.

### Build
Individual/team projects, labs, systems, applications, practical work.

### Ship
Finish, test, document, deploy, present, receive feedback, iterate.

Professional workflow should include where relevant:
- requirements;
- Agile;
- Git/version control;
- collaboration;
- documentation;
- debugging;
- review;
- testing;
- deployment;
- presentation.
